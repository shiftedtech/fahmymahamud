From lifts to data.

Then
Lifts, escalators and travellators
Now
Data pipelines, dashboards and automations

I spent 9+ years in engineering operations and facilities.

At SMRT I kept lifts and escalators running for Singapore's rail network. I was promoted to Assistant Engineer II, and I stepped up as team lead on short notice to clear a backlog of undone documentation by priority.

That's where I saw how much more I could do with data and AI.

So I retrained through Generation Singapore's Junior Data Engineer programme, with Microsoft and Temasek Polytechnic. Now I build data pipelines and AI automations that save small businesses hours of manual work.
